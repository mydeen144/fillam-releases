# Fillam releases

Fillam fills government, exam and job application forms in one click, in Tamil and English.
Your details stay on your computer. Website: **https://fillam.vercel.app**

## Install

**Microsoft Edge (recommended):** [Get Fillam from Edge Add-ons](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/cenogbihfhaifgdbcebcgfleogkldjoj). Updates come on their own.

**Google Chrome:**

1. Download **fillam.zip** from the [latest release](https://github.com/mydeen144/fillam-releases/releases/latest) and unzip it to a folder you will keep.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and switch on **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the unzipped folder.
4. Pin Fillam from the puzzle icon in the toolbar.
